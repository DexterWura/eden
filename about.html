About Eden

The startup directory for discoverability and growth.

What we do

Eden is a curated directory of startups. We help founders get discovered by investors, customers, and partners—and help everyone else find the next wave of innovation.

For founders

Submit your startup once. It appears in search, categories, and—if you’re launching today—on the Launching today page. No paywall for a basic listing.

For visitors

Browse by category, search by name or tag, and check the Launching today page for fresh launches. Subscribe to the weekly digest to stay updated.

Guidelines

  • Your startup must be real and operational (no placeholders).
  • Provide a clear description and link.
  • One listing per startup. Updates are free.
